**1. Academic Performance**



**Academic Performance means how well the student is doing in their studies.**



It can include things like:



Exam marks

Subject performance

Internal assessments

Overall academic results



In your dataset, it is represented as a score from 1 to 10.



Example:



Academic Performance = 9 → very good academic performance

Academic Performance = 5 → average performance

Academic Performance = 2 → low performance



**2. Extra-Curricular Score**



**Give a score based on participation and achievements in:**



Extra-Curricular Score means how much a student participates in activities outside regular academics, such as:



Sports

Cultural activities

Clubs

NSS/NCC

Volunteering

Competitions

College events



In your dataset, this is represented as a score from 0 to 10.



For example:



Extra-Curricular Score = 8 → the student has a relatively high level of participation.

Extra-Curricular Score = 2 → relatively low participation.
